About Sfinco

Sfinco takes its name from the Sphinx, the ancient guardian remembered for protecting the wise and challenging anyone who meant harm. That's the idea the whole business is built around: protecting people who don't know they need protecting, whether that's a small business owner without anyone watching their systems, or someone quietly unsure about a text message that didn't feel quite right. Sfinco works across four connected areas from the Sunshine Coast: plain-English cyber audits for small businesses, an eight-book digital safety guide series, a personal cyber awareness assistant currently in development, and a digital vault for passwords and important documents further down the track.

Sfinco isn't a call centre and it isn't a tech company. It's a small, local, remote-first operation built on the idea that staying safer online shouldn't require a computer science degree to understand. No scare tactics, no jargon left unexplained, and nothing pushed on anyone who doesn't need it yet. Get in touch through any page on this site and you'll hear back personally, usually within a couple of business days, from right here on the Sunshine Coast.

Who's behind it

About Leo

Leo works as a Customer Banking Specialist on the Sunshine Coast, which means a fair few of his working days start with someone telling him about a call, a text, or an email that didn't feel quite right. Sfinco grew out of noticing the same handful of tricks come up again and again, and wanting somewhere to point people that wasn't a lecture.

He's RG146 and RG206 qualified (the industry training that lets bank staff actually advise customers, not just process forms), holds a CompTIA Security+ certification, and is completing a Certificate IV in Cyber Security alongside a growing interest in financial crime investigation. On weekends he volunteers with CoderDojo, teaching local kids the basics of coding, which is more or less where the idea that anyone can learn this stuff, at any age, comes from.

Sfinco is built alongside his banking career, not instead of it, one evening and weekend at a time.
