The Sfinco Safety Guides

Digital safety, explained the way a friend would explain it.

Eight short books, one topic each, written in plain English with no assumed knowledge. No fear tactics, no tech jargon, just the settings worth checking and why they matter.

Out first

Volume 1: iPhone Protection

Sfinco Safety Guides · Vol. 1 iPhone Protection

Your iPhone holds your banking apps, your photos, your messages to family, and most people have never been shown how to lock it down properly. This guide walks through it step by step: your passcode and Face ID, Apple ID and iCloud security, which app permissions are worth worrying about, spotting scam texts and calls, safer Wi-Fi habits, and what to do the moment a phone goes missing.

Each chapter ends with a short checklist, and the back of the book includes a plain-English glossary and a tearout quick-reference card for the fridge or the wallet.
