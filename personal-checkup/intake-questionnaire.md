# Personal Digital Check-up — Intake Conversation

Internal script for Stage 1 (`methodology.md`) — a relaxed conversation, not a form. If this is a Family Digital Safety Check, this first call is usually with the adult child; see `family-framing.md` before running it that way.

## Before the review gets booked

- What device(s) does the person mainly use — iPhone, Android, a laptop, more than one?
- Is there anything specific that's prompted looking into this — a strange call, a text that felt off, an account that got locked, or just wanting some peace of mind?
- Who else is involved — is this being booked by the person themselves, or by someone else on their behalf? If someone else: has the person whose phone this is agreed to it? (See `family-framing.md` — this needs a genuine yes before Stage 2 is booked.)
- Any particular apps or accounts that matter most day to day — banking app, email, something they use to stay in touch with family?

## What to explain up front

- This is a conversation, not a test — there's no such thing as a wrong answer, and most people are doing better than they think.
- It takes about 30–45 minutes, done together on a call, looking at the actual phone.
- The price is fixed and confirmed now, before anything else happens.

## Output

A rough sense of what to focus on during the review, and the fixed price agreed before Stage 2 is booked in.
