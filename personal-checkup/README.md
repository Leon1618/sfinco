# Personal Digital Check-up / Family Digital Safety Check — product build

One product, sold two ways — a plain-English review of an individual's phone and accounts, aimed at Sfinco's actual core audience (people, not businesses) rather than the SMB Cyber Audits pillar. Separate from `smb-audits/`, but built the same way: internal working docs here, marketing pages on the live site.

- [`methodology.md`](methodology.md) — the three-stage process (free conversation → 30–45 min review → one-page summary), the six review areas mirrored from Sfinco Guides Vol 1 (locking the front door, Apple ID/iCloud, app permissions, scams, Wi-Fi, Find My), and the dignity-first principle running through all of it.
- [`family-framing.md`](family-framing.md) — what changes between the Personal Digital Check-up and the Family Digital Safety Check (same product, different buyer): who's on the first call, the marketing angle, and the hard rule that the parent is always part of the actual review, never reviewed *about*.
- [`intake-questionnaire.md`](intake-questionnaire.md) — the Stage 1 conversation script.
- [`pricing.md`](pricing.md) — suggested flat $99 AUD, one-off, no worksheet needed.
- [`report-template.md`](report-template.md) — the one-page client summary, duplicate per client.
