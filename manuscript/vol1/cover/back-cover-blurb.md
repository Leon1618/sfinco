# iPhone Protection, Back Cover Copy

**Blurb:**

Your iPhone holds your bank details, your family's photos, and messages you'd never want a stranger to see. Most people have never been shown how to lock it down properly, or which settings actually matter versus which are just noise.

This is a plain-English, step-by-step guide to protecting your iPhone, written for adults who don't want to be lectured, don't want jargon, and just want to feel confident. Real screenshots. Real Australian scam examples. No technical background required.

**In this book:**

- Setting up a passcode and Face ID properly
- Locking down your Apple ID, the master key to everything
- Checking which apps can see your location, camera, and contacts
- Spotting the scam texts and calls currently targeting Australians
- Staying safe on public Wi-Fi
- Setting up Find My so a lost phone is a hassle, not a disaster
- A 15-minute monthly checkup to keep it that way

**Author bio (short):**

Leonardo Pinheiro works in banking and technology on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland, holding qualifications in cybersecurity, financial advising, and information technology. He wrote this series after years of watching capable, careful people get caught out by scams nobody had ever warned them about.
