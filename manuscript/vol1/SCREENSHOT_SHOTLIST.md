## Before you start

1. **Turn on Do Not Disturb / Focus** so no notification banners appear mid-screenshot.
2. **Check the status bar looks clean**: full-ish battery, Wi-Fi connected, no low-battery red icon.
3. **Take the screenshot**: press the Side button and Volume Up together, release immediately. It saves to Photos.
4. **Transfer to your Mac**: AirDrop each batch to yourself, or use the Photos app's iCloud sync, into a folder. Doesn't matter which, just tell me where and I'll pick them up from there.
5. **Naming doesn't matter**: send them in order (e.g. by chapter) and I'll rename and place them to match the manuscript's existing filenames myself.
