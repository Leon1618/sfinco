### iPhone Protection


Your simple guide to staying safe online


# Introduction


Let's be honest — nobody wakes up in the morning thinking: "I should probably review my iPhone's privacy settings today."

Life is busy. Technology is confusing. And the people who design scams are unfortunately very good at what they do.

That is why this guide exists.

This is not a technical manual. There are no complicated diagrams, no long lists of jargon, and no assumption that you already know what a "VPN" is or why "two-factor authentication" sounds like something from a spy film. (It isn't — and by the end of this book, you will wonder how you ever went without it.)

What this guide is, is a plain-English, step-by-step walkthrough of the most important things you can do right now to protect yourself on your iPhone. Each chapter tackles one area of your digital life, explains why it matters in everyday terms, and then shows you — with real screenshots — exactly what to do and where to find it.

Whether you are someone who has had an iPhone for years and never thought much about security, or someone who recently heard a story about a friend getting scammed and wants to make sure it never happens to you, this book is written for you.


## Who is this book for?


This guide is for anyone who uses an iPhone and wants to feel confident that their personal information, their money, and their family are protected. You do not need to be "tech-savvy." You just need to be willing to spend a few hours going through these steps — most of which take less than two minutes each.

In particular, this guide will be helpful if you:

have ever received a suspicious phone call, text message, or email and wondered whether it was real

are not entirely sure what your Apple ID is or why it matters so much

share an iPhone plan with family members and want to keep everyone safe

have a parent, grandparent, or older relative who uses an iPhone and worries about online scams

simply want peace of mind — knowing your phone is locked down and your information is protected


## A quick word from the author


I work in banking and technology on the Sunshine Coast in Queensland, and I spend a lot of time helping people — particularly older Australians — navigate the digital world safely. What I have seen over and over again is not stupidity or carelessness. It is a gap.

The people who design scams and cyberattacks are professionals. They are well-funded, creative, and relentless. Meanwhile, most of us were never taught the basics of digital self-defence. We were handed a smartphone and told to get on with it.

This guide is my attempt to close that gap — one iPhone at a time.

The steps in this book are the same ones I walk through with my own family members. They are practical, they are proven, and they do not require any special skills. All you need is your iPhone, a bit of time, and this book.

Let's get started.


# How to use this book


This book is designed to be used, not just read. Here is how to get the most out of it.


## Work through it chapter by chapter


Each chapter builds gently on the one before it, starting with the most fundamental protections (your passcode and Face ID) and working through to more advanced topics (Wi-Fi safety and what to do if your phone is ever lost or stolen). If you are new to iPhone security, the best approach is to start at Chapter 1 and work your way through.

That said, every chapter also stands on its own. If there is a specific topic that is urgent for you right now — scam texts, for example, or backing up your phone — feel free to jump straight there.


## Do the steps as you go


This is not a book to read in bed and then forget about. It is a book to read with your iPhone in your hand. Each chapter includes step-by-step instructions with screenshots showing exactly what you will see on your screen. Work through each step as you read it, and by the time you finish a chapter, that protection is already in place.

*  TIP:  If you find a step confusing or your screen looks different from the screenshots, don't panic. iOS updates occasionally change where settings live. A quick search online for the setting name will usually point you in the right direction — or ask someone you trust for help.


## Use the checklist at the back


The final chapter of this book contains a one-page security checklist you can use every month to make sure your iPhone stays protected over time. Tear it out. Stick it on the fridge. Set a reminder on your calendar for the first Sunday of every month. Five minutes a month is all it takes to stay on top of things.


## Share it with someone you care about


If you know someone — a parent, a neighbour, a friend — who could benefit from this guide, please share it with them. Scammers target people who are isolated and uninformed. The more people in your circle who know these basics, the safer everyone is.


## A note on screenshots and iOS versions


The screenshots in this book were taken using iOS 17. If you are using a slightly older or newer version of iOS, some screens may look a little different — but the core settings and steps are almost always in the same place.

To check which version of iOS you are running: go to Settings > General > About, and look for "Software Version." If your version is significantly older than iOS 16, consider updating before working through this guide, as some features may not be available on older software.

!  IMPORTANT:  Keeping your iPhone updated is one of the single most important things you can do for your security. Apple regularly releases updates that fix known vulnerabilities. We cover this in detail in Chapter 8.


## What this book does not cover


This guide focuses specifically on your iPhone. It does not cover Mac computers, Android phones, or general internet safety beyond what relates directly to your device. If you are interested in those topics, they are covered in other volumes in the Sfinco Guides series.

This guide also does not go deep into corporate or business-level security. If you run a small business and want to protect your team and your systems, take a look at the Sfinco Guide for Small Business — coming soon in Series 2.
