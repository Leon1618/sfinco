## Two new habits worth adding


Keeping iOS up to date

Apple regularly releases iOS updates that patch newly discovered security vulnerabilities. Running an outdated version of iOS means your phone may be missing protection against threats that have already been fixed for everyone who has updated.

Settings path:  Settings  >  General  >  Software Update

Check this screen during your monthly review. If an update is available, connect to Wi-Fi, plug your phone in to charge, and install it. For convenience, you can also turn on Automatic Updates so your phone installs important updates overnight without you needing to remember.

> **★ TIP**
>
> Settings > General > Software Update > Automatic Updates. Turn on both 'Install iOS Updates' and 'Security Responses & System Files'. Your phone will then install critical security updates automatically, usually overnight while charging — no action needed from you.



Keeping your apps up to date

App updates often include security fixes in addition to new features. By default, most iPhones update apps automatically, but it is worth confirming this setting and doing a manual check during your monthly review.

Settings path:  Settings  >  App Store  >  App Updates (toggle on)

To check for and install any pending updates manually, open the App Store, tap your profile icon in the top right, and scroll down to see any apps with updates available.
