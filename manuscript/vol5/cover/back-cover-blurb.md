# Android Protection, Back Cover Copy

**Blurb:**

Your Android phone holds your banking apps, your messages, and your Google account, the master key to almost everything else in your digital life. Most people have never been shown which settings actually protect it.

This is a plain-English, step-by-step guide to securing your Android phone, written for adults who don't want jargon and just want to feel confident. Real screenshots. Real Australian scam examples. No technical background required.

**In this book:**

- Setting up your screen lock, fingerprint, and encryption properly
- Locking down your Google account and turning on Play Protect
- Checking which apps can see your location, camera, and contacts
- Spotting the scam texts and calls currently targeting Australians
- Staying safe on public Wi-Fi
- What to do if your phone is ever lost or stolen
- A 15-minute monthly checkup to keep it that way

**Author bio (short):**

Leonardo Pinheiro works in banking and technology on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland, holding qualifications in cybersecurity, financial advising, and information technology. He wrote this series after years of watching capable, careful people get caught out by scams nobody had ever warned them about.
