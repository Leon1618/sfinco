# Windows Protection, Back Cover Copy

**Blurb:**

Windows runs on more computers than anything else in the world, which is exactly why it's the single most targeted platform for scams and viruses. Most people have never been shown which settings actually matter.

This is a plain-English, step-by-step guide to securing your Windows PC, written for adults who don't want jargon and just want to feel confident at their own desk. Clear illustrations. Real Australian scam examples. No technical background required.

**In this book:**

- Setting up your PIN, Windows Hello, and device encryption
- Understanding your Microsoft account and OneDrive backup
- Getting the most out of Windows Defender and app permissions
- Spotting fake pop-ups, phishing emails, and tech support scams
- Firewall and VPN basics for public Wi-Fi
- What to do if your PC is ever lost, stolen, or hit by ransomware
- A 15-minute monthly checkup to keep it that way

**Author bio (short):**

Leonardo Pinheiro works in banking and technology on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland, holding qualifications in cybersecurity, financial advising, and information technology. He wrote this series after years of watching capable, careful people get caught out by scams nobody had ever warned them about.
