# MacBook Protection, Back Cover Copy

**Blurb:**

Your Mac holds decades of photos, documents, and passwords, often signed in and ready to go the moment someone opens the lid. Most people have never been shown which settings actually protect that, and which are just there to look impressive.

This is a plain-English, step-by-step guide to securing your Mac, written for adults who don't want jargon and just want to feel confident sitting down at their own computer. Real screenshots. Real Australian scam examples. No technical background required.

**In this book:**

- Setting up your login and FileVault encryption properly
- Understanding Gatekeeper and safe app downloads
- Locking down your Apple ID and iCloud
- Spotting fake tech support calls and phishing emails
- Firewall and VPN basics for public Wi-Fi
- Time Machine backups that actually work when you need them
- A 15-minute monthly checkup to keep it that way

**Author bio (short):**

Leonardo Pinheiro works in banking and technology on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland, holding qualifications in cybersecurity, financial advising, and information technology. He wrote this series after years of watching capable, careful people get caught out by scams nobody had ever warned them about.
