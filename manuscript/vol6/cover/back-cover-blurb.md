# Small Business Protection — Back Cover Copy

**Blurb:**

You don't need to be a big company to be worth attacking. Small businesses are targeted constantly, precisely because they rarely have the layers of checking a larger company does, and a single successful scam can do genuine, lasting damage.

This is a plain-English, step-by-step guide to protecting your business, written for owners and sole traders who don't have an IT department and don't have time for jargon. Real examples. No technical background required.

**In this book:**

- Passwords, password managers, and multi-factor authentication for your whole team
- Email security and protecting your business from being impersonated
- Staff access, and the offboarding step almost every business forgets
- Spotting invoice fraud and the scams currently targeting Australian businesses
- Wi-Fi, point-of-sale, and remote access for staff working from anywhere
- Backups and a calm, practical plan if something goes wrong
- A 15-minute monthly checkup to keep it that way

**Author bio (short):**

Leonardo Pinheiro works in banking and technology on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland, holding qualifications in cybersecurity, financial advising, and information technology. He also offers remote SMB Cyber Audits for Sunshine Coast small businesses through Sfinco.
