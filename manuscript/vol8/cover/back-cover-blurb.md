# Personal Finance Protection — Back Cover Copy

**Blurb:**

Every device, password, and habit in this series eventually connects back to the same thing: your money. This final volume is about protecting your banking, your superannuation, and your savings from the specific scams built to target them.

This is a plain-English, step-by-step guide, written for adults who want to feel confident with their finances without being sold anything. It is not financial advice. It's about recognising the scams and habits that put your money at risk, and knowing exactly where to turn when a decision genuinely needs a licensed professional.

**In this book:**

- Locking down your everyday banking app, alerts, and card controls
- Protecting your myGov account and superannuation from scams
- Password managers and safer habits across every financial account
- Recognising investment and romance scams before they cost you
- Safer online shopping, digital wallets, and buy now pay later
- Wills, powers of attorney, and making sure your family can find your accounts
- A 15-minute monthly checkup to keep it that way

**Author bio (short):**

Leonardo Pinheiro is a Customer Banking Specialist on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland, holding RG146 accreditation and qualifications in cybersecurity and information technology. This book, and the series it completes, grew out of years spent helping people recognise the warning signs nobody had pointed out to them in time.
