# Family and Home Network Protection, Back Cover Copy

**Blurb:**

Every phone, laptop, smart TV, and camera in your house connects through the same router, most of it still running whatever settings it arrived with. One weak link is often all it takes.

This is a plain-English, step-by-step guide to protecting your whole household online, written for parents and grandparents who want everyone under their roof kept safe without needing to become the family IT department. Real screenshots. Real Australian scam examples. No technical background required.

**In this book:**

- Securing your router and changing the settings everyone forgets
- Setting up a separate guest network for visitors and smart devices
- Checking exactly what's connected to your Wi-Fi right now
- Locking down smart TVs, cameras, and other household devices
- Age-appropriate parental controls that don't require constant policing
- Spotting the scams currently targeting Australian families
- A 15-minute monthly checkup to keep it that way

**Author bio (short):**

Leonardo Pinheiro works in banking and technology on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland, holding qualifications in cybersecurity, financial advising, and information technology. He wrote this series after years of watching capable, careful people get caught out by scams nobody had ever warned them about.
