# NGO and Not-for-Profit Protection, Back Cover Copy

**Blurb:**

Charities and community organisations carry a particular kind of trust, and that trust is exactly what makes them worth targeting. Most run on volunteers and committee members with no IT department and no spare budget for security.

This is a plain-English, step-by-step guide to protecting your organisation, written for volunteers and committee members who don't have time for jargon and want a genuinely free, practical starting point. Real examples. No technical background required.

**In this book:**

- Passwords, free password managers, and multi-factor authentication for your committee
- Free and discounted software available to registered charities
- Volunteer and committee access, and what to do at every handover
- Spotting cloned donation pages and the scams currently targeting Australian charities
- Wi-Fi, shared devices, and remote access for a team that's always changing
- Donor data protection and a calm, practical plan if something goes wrong
- A 15-minute monthly checkup to keep it that way

**Author bio (short):**

Leonardo Pinheiro works in banking and technology on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland, holding qualifications in cybersecurity, financial advising, and information technology. He volunteers with CoderDojo and has a background in community technology education.
