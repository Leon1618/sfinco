# Business Context (reference only — not for v1 of the website)

## Regulatory framework — always flag when relevant

Any content or feature touching financial advice, investment recommendations, Open Banking data, consumer credit, insurance, or superannuation must flag the relevant Australian regulatory framework before proceeding:

- **CDR (Consumer Data Right)** — ACCC accreditation required for Open Banking data access.
- **AFSL (Australian Financial Services Licence)** — required for financial advice.
- **RG146 / RG206** — ASIC regulatory guidance for financial product advisers. Leo holds both.
- **Privacy Act 1988** — data handling obligations for personal and financial information.
- **Corporations Act 2001** — financial services and product disclosure.

This applies directly to any SfincoAssist Phase 2 content on the site, and to any page that discusses handling users' financial data (including the Digital Estate Vault).
