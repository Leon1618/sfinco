Coming soon

A financial companion that remembers, so you don't have to.

Sfinco Personal Assistant is being built to quietly keep track of the money details that are easy to lose in the shuffle: a bonus interest rate about to drop, a term deposit about to mature, a subscription that's crept up. It isn't live yet, but here's what it's going to do.

First version

A gentle reminder engine, not a bank connection

The first version of Sfinco Personal Assistant won't connect to your bank at all. You tell it what to watch, and it nudges you before the date arrives. No bank login, no shared passwords, just a companion that remembers so it doesn't all have to live in your head or on a scrap of paper.

  • 1 Bonus interest rate step-downs
  • 2 Term deposit maturity dates
  • 3 Loan and credit card repayment due dates
  • 4 Subscription and direct debit changes
Further down the track

The bigger idea

Eventually, Sfinco Personal Assistant may connect directly to your accounts through Open Banking, so it can compare rates and flag better options automatically instead of waiting for you to enter details by hand. It would still be a companion, not an advisor: something that surfaces information so you can make your own call, not something that tells you what to do with your money.

Why this isn't live yet

Connecting to real banking data requires accreditation under Australia's Consumer Data Right (CDR) through the ACCC, and depending on what Sfinco Personal Assistant eventually recommends, it may also need an Australian Financial Services Licence (AFSL). Sfinco takes these requirements seriously. Nothing on this site connects to your bank accounts, and Sfinco Personal Assistant won't handle real financial data until the right accreditation is in place.
