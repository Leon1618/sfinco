Who it's for

Built for businesses without an IT department

Cafes, clinics, real estate offices, trades, and professional services across Noosa and the Sunshine Coast: anyone running a business on laptops, phones, and cloud software without a dedicated IT person keeping an eye on things.

How it works

The audit process

  • A short intake conversation

    We talk through how your business uses technology: email, payments, customer data, and the software your team relies on day to day. No forms full of jargon, just a conversation.

  • A remote risk review

    We check the areas that matter most: account security, backups, software updates, and how customer and payment data is handled. All done remotely, without disrupting your day.

  • A plain-English report

    You get a written report that explains what we found and why it matters, in language built for a business owner, not an IT team.

  • A clear list of fixes

    Every finding comes with a practical next step, ranked by what to tackle first. An ongoing retainer is available if you'd like Sfinco to help you work through the list, but it's never required.

Pricing

What it costs

Most audits run between $500 and $2,000 AUD, depending on the size of your business and how many systems are in scope. You'll get a fixed quote after the intake conversation, before any work begins.

No obligation, no pressure

The intake conversation is free and doesn't commit you to anything. If an audit isn't the right fit for your business right now, we'll say so.

After the audit

Staying switched on, not just fixed once

An audit gives you a clear picture on the day we run it. Cyber Awareness is the ongoing side: a plain-English score you can watch move over time, and a short monthly note to keep your team a step ahead, without adding another thing to dread in the inbox.

Your Cyber Confidence Score

Scored off the same checklist your audit used: securing your accounts, protecting your devices, and preparing your staff. No certificate, no jargon, just a plain read on where things stand, updated at each check-in.
