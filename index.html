About Leo

L

Leo lives on the Sunshine Coast and works as a Customer Banking Specialist and Digital Concierge, helping everyday people navigate their money and avoid scams. He's RG146 and RG206 qualified, holds a CompTIA Security+ certification, and is completing a Certificate IV in Cyber Security alongside running Sfinco.

He also volunteers his time with CoderDojo, helping local kids get their first taste of coding. Sfinco grew out of a simple observation from years of banking conversations: most people aren't careless, they're just never been shown what to check. Sfinco is Leo's way of showing them, one audit, one guide, one honest conversation at a time.

A note on what's live today

SMB Cyber Audits are open for booking now, and the first Sfinco Guide is on its way. SfincoAssist and the Digital Estate Vault are still in development. Nothing on this site connects to your bank accounts or financial data. When those tools do launch, anything involving your money will always meet the relevant Australian regulatory requirements first.
