Personal Digital Check-up · internal

Check-up dashboard

The lighter counterpart to the SMB Cyber Audit Dashboard — same idea, three stages instead of six. Works for both framings: a Personal Digital Check-up booked directly, or a Family Digital Safety Check booked by an adult child. Delivery is identical either way; see family-framing.md for what actually changes (who's on the first call, never who's in the review).

The three stages

  • 1. Conversation — free, ~20 min. What's prompted this, what device they use, price confirmed ($99 AUD flat, not yet public — see pricing.md).
  • 2. Review — 30–45 min, done together on a call. The six areas from Sfinco Guides Vol 1, walked through live.
  • 3. Report — a one-page summary, not a multi-page document. Two or three things worth doing, and what's already going well.

Dignity first

Not "help for someone who can't keep up." Nobody is condescended to, on the call, in the report, or when the buyer is an adult child talking about a parent who isn't there. If a sentence could read as talking down to someone, rewrite it — same rule whether it's Leo writing or this tool prefilling something.
