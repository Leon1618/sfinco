SMB Cyber Audits · internal

The audit playbook

Start to finish, in order. Written so anyone stepping into this part of Sfinco — including a future employee who's never run an audit — can follow it without asking what comes next.

Before taking a paying client

  • Sfinco's ABN and bank details filled into smb-audits/invoicing.md and smb-audits/engagement-agreement-template.md — currently placeholders.
  • GST registration status confirmed — decides how every invoice is worded.
  • The Limitation of liability clause in the engagement agreement reviewed by a solicitor — not ready to send as-is.
  • Professional indemnity insurance looked into.
1

Intake conversation

Free · 20–30 min, phone or video

A genuine "tell me about your business" conversation, not a sales call. Ends with a scope note and a fixed quote sent within 24 hours.

  • Read first: methodology.md (Principles + Stage 1), intake-questionnaire.md
  • Work through the ACSC-based 15-item checklist live on the call
  • Straight after: turn the business details into a fixed quote
Intake Call Tool → Pricing & Scoping Calculator →
Before the review is booked in: send the engagement agreement and the client data handling notice (Stage 1.5 below).
1.5

Agreement & privacy notice

Same day as the quote, before the review begins

Not one of the five methodology stages, but has to happen here — the review shouldn't start without a signed agreement in hand.

  • Read first: engagement-agreement-template.md, privacy-notice.md
  • Fill in the domains agreed, the quote, and the timeline
  • Send the privacy notice alongside — building trust starts here, not at delivery
Engagement Agreement Generator →
The liability clause always renders as a flagged placeholder — that's deliberate, see Before taking a paying client above.
2

Remote risk review

~15–20 min self-check (client's time) + 30–45 min verify call — down from the old 2–4 hour version

Two parts: a guided self-check the client does on their own for what they can honestly self-report, then a short focused call for what needs your eye directly (MFA, admin access, payment handling), plus anything that came back unclear. See review-item-modes.md for which of the 20 checks lands where.

  • Read first: methodology.md (Assessment domains), review-item-modes.md
  • Send the client: self-check.html on the live site links to the real self-check tool once they've booked
  • Rate the verify call live in the Dashboard's Review panel
Guided Self-Check → Remote Review checklist →
3–4

Findings, rating & the report

Written up after the review, delivered on a final call

Every finding: what we found → why it matters → what to do, rated Fix now / Fix soon / Good to know. Bundled into a report an owner can read in 15–20 minutes.

  • Read first: report-template.md
  • Reference: findings-library.md for canned Yes/No language per item
  • Send the invoice alongside the report
Audit Report Generator →
Invoicing: invoicing.md — no dedicated tool, it's a fill-in template.
5

Optional retainer

Offered once, plainly, on the final call · flat $99 AUD/month

Never a condition of the audit, never chased afterward. If taken up, this is what Cyber Awareness actually delivers.

  • Read first: cyber-awareness.md
  • Delivers: the Cyber Confidence Score re-checked at each catch-up, the monthly Sfinco Check-in note, and a manual monthly Breach Check via haveibeenpwned.com
Retainer reference → Delivery itself stays manual, by design, until there's real retainer volume to justify tooling.
