Who it's for

Anyone who wants a second pair of eyes

Whether you've had a strange call or text lately, want a bit of peace of mind, or just haven't looked at your phone's settings since you bought it — this is for you, at any age. There's no such thing as a silly question here.

How it works

The check-up process

  • A free, relaxed conversation

    About 20 minutes, by phone or video. What device you use, anything that's prompted looking into this, and what matters most to you day to day. No jargon, just a chat.

  • The check-up itself

    30–45 minutes, done together on a call, looking at your actual phone. Locking the front door, your main account's security, app permissions, spotting a scam, safe Wi-Fi habits, and what to do if your phone's ever lost.

  • A one-page summary

    Two or three things worth doing, in plain English, plus what's already going well — because something usually is. Sent by email, and talked through on the call itself.

What the six areas cover

Locking the front door (passcode, Face ID, auto-lock) · your Apple ID or Google account · app permissions · spotting scams and phishing · safe Wi-Fi habits · Find My and what to do if your phone's lost. The same ground as Sfinco's iPhone Protection guide, as a conversation instead of a book.
