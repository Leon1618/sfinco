# SMB Cyber Audit — Pricing & Scoping Worksheet

Internal tool. Fill this in straight after the Stage 1 intake call ([`intake-questionnaire.md`](intake-questionnaire.md)) to turn what you just heard into a fixed quote, before booking Stage 2. Not shown to clients — they get the number, not the working.

Start at the base fee, add what applies, then round and apply the floor/ceiling below.

*Repriced from the original $500–$2,000 band once Stage 2 dropped from a 2–4 hour live screen-share to a ~15–20 min self-check plus a 30–45 min verify call — the old numbers reflected a heavier delivery than the audit actually costs to run now. See `review-item-modes.md` for what changed.*

## Base fee

**$299** — covers a simple, single-location business: a small team, a handful of devices, no card data handled directly, one or two everyday software services.

## Add for scope

| Factor | Add | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Team size 3–6 people | +$75 | vs the 1–2 person baseline |
| Team size 7–15 people | +$175 | more accounts and devices to review |
| Team size 16+ | — | see *When it doesn't fit* below |
| 5–15 devices in scope | +$75 | business-owned + BYO used for work |
| 16–30 devices in scope | +$150 | |
| 31+ devices in scope | — | see *When it doesn't fit* below |
| Each software/service beyond the first two | +$35 | POS, booking system, accounting, cloud storage, industry-specific software — email doesn't count as one of the first two, everyone has it |
| Takes card payments directly, not through a compliant third party (Square, Stripe, EFTPOS) | +$100 | extra time reviewing how card data actually moves and where it's stored |
| Holds sensitive customer data (health, financial, legal records) | +$75 | higher-stakes data handling review |
| Website in scope for review | +$50 | on top of the core domains |
| More than one location, or a distributed/remote team | +$75 | coordination overhead, more environments to check |

## Round and apply the limits

- Round the total to the nearest $25.
- **Floor: $299.** Never quote below this, even for a very small business — it's not worth the time otherwise.
- **Ceiling: $799.** If the add-ons push the total past this, don't quote a number over $799 — see below instead.

## When it doesn't fit

If team size lands at 16+, devices at 31+, or the running total would clear $799: don't force a number. Either scope it down with the client (fewer systems in this round, a follow-up audit later for the rest) or treat it as a custom engagement and quote separately, outside the standard $299–$799 band. Say so plainly on the call — "this one's bigger than our usual scope, let me put together a proper quote" is a fine thing to say.

## Worked example

**Noosa Coffee Co.** — café, 4 staff, three services beyond email (POS, accounting software, cloud storage), card payments through Square (a compliant third party, not handled directly), no sensitive-data category, single location, no website in scope.

- Base: $299
- Team size 3–6: +$75
- Services beyond the first two (POS + accounting counted, cloud storage is the 1 extra): +$35
- Card payments via Square: no add — third-party processor, not direct handling
- Running total: $409 → rounded to nearest $25: **$400**

Quote given to the client: a flat **$400 AUD**, before any work begins — matches the "fixed quote after the intake conversation" promise on `audits.html`.
