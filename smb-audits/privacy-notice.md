# SMB Cyber Audit — Client Data Handling Notice

Plain-English notice to give clients alongside the engagement agreement, explaining what happens to their information during an audit. Send it early — it's part of building the trust the audit depends on, not just paperwork.

## Where Sfinco likely stands under the Privacy Act

As a sole trader with turnover under $3 million, Sfinco most likely falls under the **Privacy Act 1988**'s small business operator exemption, meaning the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) don't strictly apply. Two things narrow that, worth knowing rather than assuming away:

- **Health service providers are never exempt**, regardless of turnover. If an audit client is a clinic or health-adjacent business and Sfinco ends up handling any patient data directly (unlikely in the normal course of an audit, but worth watching for), the exemption doesn't cover that.
- The exemption covers *Sfinco's* obligations. It says nothing about the *client's own* Privacy Act obligations for their customer data — which is exactly what the "Payment and customer data handling" domain of the audit is assessing.

This notice is written and followed regardless of whether the exemption technically applies — it's what a client should reasonably expect, and it matches the brand promise better than leaning on a legal exemption.

## What gets collected

- Contact and business details given at intake (name, business name, email, what the business does).
- What's observed during the remote review — account and device configuration, security settings, general practices. Never passwords, card numbers, or account credentials; nothing is ever asked for or typed into anything Sfinco controls.
- Notes taken to write the report.

## What doesn't get collected

No client passwords, no card or bank details, no copies of customer databases. If something like that appears on screen during a review by accident, it's not recorded or referenced in the report beyond noting that it shouldn't be visible there.

## Where it's kept

Notes and the finished report are kept on Leo's own device, not uploaded to any third-party platform beyond the email used to send the report itself. Nothing is shared with any other business, contractor, or data broker.

## How long it's kept

Kept for 12 months after the report is delivered, in case the client wants a follow-up audit or a comparison over time — then deleted. A client can ask for earlier deletion at any point.

## Client rights

Any client can ask to see what's held about them, correct anything that's wrong, or have it deleted early. Email hello@sfinco.com.au for any of this.
