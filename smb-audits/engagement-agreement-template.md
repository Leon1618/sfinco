# SMB Cyber Audit — Engagement Agreement Template

**Not legal advice, and not ready to send as-is.** This is a plain-English starting draft covering the terms a small-business cyber audit engagement typically needs. Before it's used with a real client — especially the **Limitation of liability** section — have a solicitor look it over, or run it through a template service aimed at small businesses (LawPath and Legal123 both do fixed-fee small-business contract reviews; the Sunshine Coast Community Legal Service may also point you to low-cost options). This is the one document in `smb-audits/` where getting it wrong has real financial exposure, so treat the draft as a head start, not a finished contract.

**Also worth doing before the first paying client:** look into professional indemnity insurance. If a client ever relies on this report and something goes wrong that the audit didn't catch, indemnity cover is what stands between that and your own money. Your RG146/RG206 background means you already know this instinct from banking — it applies here too.

---

## Cyber Audit Engagement Agreement

**Between:** Sfinco (Leo Tavares, ABN [your ABN]) — "Sfinco"
**And:** [Client business name] ("the Client")
**Date:** [date]

### 1. Services

Sfinco will carry out an SMB Cyber Audit for the Client, covering the domains agreed at intake:

[List domains from the scoping calculator — e.g. Identity and access, Email and phishing exposure, Devices and software, Backups and recovery, Payment and customer data handling, Network]

The audit follows the process set out in Sfinco's audit methodology: an intake conversation, a remote risk review, a written report with rated findings, and a ranked list of recommended fixes.

### 2. What's not included

This audit is a risk and practice review, not a penetration test, not a formal compliance certification against any named standard (ISO 27001, PCI DSS, Essential Eight, or otherwise), and not financial or investment advice. It does not include implementing any of the recommended fixes — that's covered separately if the Client takes up the optional retainer.

### 3. Fee and payment

Fixed fee: **$[amount] AUD** [inclusive/exclusive of GST — confirm your registration status before sending any invoice].

Payment due within [7] days of the report being delivered, by direct bank transfer to the details on the invoice. [Alternative: 50% on booking, 50% on delivery, if you'd rather not carry the full engagement unpaid.]

### 4. Timeline

Sfinco will deliver the report within [X] business days of completing the remote review, scheduled by mutual agreement.

### 5. Client responsibilities

The Client will provide reasonable access for the remote review (screen-share time with whoever holds relevant admin access) and answer questions honestly and to the best of their knowledge. The audit's findings are only as good as the access and information provided.

### 6. Confidentiality

Sfinco will keep all information gathered during the audit, and the contents of the report, confidential. Nothing is shared with any third party without the Client's written consent, except where required by law. See Sfinco's client data handling notice (`smb-audits/privacy-notice.md`) for how information is stored and for how long.

### 7. Limitation of liability

*[This clause needs a solicitor's eyes before it's relied on. A typical starting shape: the report reflects a point-in-time review based on the access and information the Client provided; it is not a guarantee against future incidents; Sfinco's liability arising from the engagement is limited to the fees paid for it. Whether that's the right shape for your situation, and whether it actually holds up, is exactly what needs checking.]*

### 8. Ownership

The report is prepared for the Client's internal use. Sfinco retains the underlying methodology and may reuse general, non-identifying insights (not client-specific data or findings) in future work.

### 9. Cancellation

Either party may cancel before the remote review begins at no cost — the intake conversation is always free. [Decide your own policy for cancellation after the review has started but before the report is delivered — e.g. no charge, or a part-fee to cover time already spent.]

### 10. Agreement

Signed for Sfinco: ______________________ Date: ________

Signed for the Client: ______________________ Date: ________
